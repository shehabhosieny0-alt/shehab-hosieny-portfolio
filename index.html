I am Eng. Shehab Hosieny Hassan, a Mechanical Power Engineer (B.Sc., Zagazig University) specialized in MEP Design, LOD 400 BIM Coordination, and Technical Office Engineering across mega projects in Saudi Arabia, Egypt, and the UAE.
